# Mechanics — Kiln Court Killers

## Infection

- Zombies spread infection on contact with orb people.
- Infection progresses in stages: clean → infected → turning → zombie.
- An orb person who turns becomes a zombie and joins the threat.

## The Cure

- The only cure is the kiln's heat.
- Lure a zombie into the kiln's radius. The heat kills the infection in nearby orb people.
- The zombie dies in the process. You spend a cure to save a life.
- Heat radius and intensity depend on fuel level.

## Temperature System

- The kiln has a heat level (0–100) that decays over time.
- Fuel (wood) restores heat. Each wood unit adds a fixed amount.
- Orb people consume heat to stay alive. More saved people = faster decay.
- Below a threshold, orb people start taking damage.

## Orb People

- Start as passive survivors scattered around the court.
- On cure, they become workers with roles:
  - **Fire-tender:** slows heat decay.
  - **Wood-hauler:** finds wood on the map.
  - **Scout:** reveals zombie positions.
- Lost orb people cannot be recovered.

## Zombies

- Wander the court on set patrol paths.
- Infect on contact. Do not attack directly — infection is the threat.
- Can be lured by sound, light, or bait (future mechanic).
- Die only in the kiln's heat. Outside it, they are effectively unkillable.

## The Cold Snap

- A timed event where outside temperature drops.
- Heat decay doubles. Fuel consumption doubles.
- Zombie count increases.
- You must choose: save everyone and burn out, or triage.

## Controls (Prototype)

- Move: arrow keys / WASD
- Lure zombie: hold Shift near a zombie
- Feed kiln: press E near wood
- Check infection: hover over orb person
