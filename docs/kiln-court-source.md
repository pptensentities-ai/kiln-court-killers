## How you play

- Drag the court.
- Tap a room or an orb.
- Canvas court, with Sound, Pause, Zoom in, Zoom out.
- Speed: 1×, 2×, 4×.
- New court resets the lots.

## Rules already in the court

- Air lags the setpoint. The dial is not the room.
- The door leaks.
- Each orb fights their own house.
- Setpoints are not the same. Pell wants ice. Cinder and Sol want it hot. Marrow sits at 25°. Nix holds the commons at 21° with the door open.
