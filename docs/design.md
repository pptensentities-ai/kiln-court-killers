# Kiln Court Killers — Design Document

## Core Fantasy

You are the keeper of the last warm place in a dying court. Zombies are not just enemies — they are the only medicine. Every cure costs a life. Every rescue costs fuel. The game is about making that trade and living with it.

## Pillars

1. **Temperature is the resource.** Heat is life. Fuel is money. Wood is time.
2. **Zombies are dual-purpose.** They infect, but they also cure. You cannot simply kill them all.
3. **Orb people are your economy.** Saved ones work. Lost ones are gone forever.
4. **The cold snap is the boss.** Not a monster — a math problem with a body count.

## Tone

Quiet horror. No jump scares. The dread comes from watching the fuel gauge and the infection counter at the same time.

## Win / Lose Conditions

- **Lose:** The kiln goes cold with orb people still alive, or all orb people are lost.
- **Win:** Survive the cold snap and keep at least one orb person and the kiln burning.

## Progression

- Level 1: Learn the kiln. One zombie, three orb people, a small wood pile.
- Level 2: Two zombies, infection spreads faster, wood is scarce.
- Level 3: The cold snap. Multiple zombies, limited fuel, hard choices.

## Future Ideas

- Zombie variants: slow burners (infect slowly, cure slowly), runners (fast infection, die fast in heat).
- Orb person roles: fire-tender, wood-hauler, scout.
- A "quarantine room" mechanic — a cold room that slows infection but doesn't cure it.
- Multiplayer co-op: one player tends the kiln, one lures zombies.
