# Infection — locked 2026-10-07

Kiln Court Killers does not add a new species.

A zombie is an orb person who is infected. Same body. Same name. Same house. Wrong temperature in the blood.

## Start

- The five stay: Marrow, Pell, Sol, Cinder, Nix.
- A couple of them begin already infected.
- The rest can catch it.

## Spread

- Infected orbs move like orbs. They still belong to their room.
- Contact with a clean orb can pass the infection.
- An infected orb is not deleted. They are still themselves.

## The cure

Cook the infection out.

The infected orb has to enter their own kiln house — the room they raised — and the room has to be warm enough for that body.

- Marrow cooks in Marrow's kiln.
- Pell cooks in Pell's vault.
- Sol cooks in Sol's solarium.
- Cinder cooks in Cinder's grate.
- Nix cooks in Nix's commons.

A neighbor's house does not count. The according house is theirs.

Heat already lags the setpoint, and the door already leaks. The cure uses that. You do not flip a switch. You hold the room hot long enough that the infection cooks off while they are inside.

## What this does to the old court

- Cold snap is worse: rooms fall off the cure temperature.
- Heat wave can help a cure and hurt an orb who wanted ice.
- Pell is the hard case. Their setpoint is 14°. Cooking them means pushing the vault hotter than they want, on purpose.
- Nix's door is open. The commons leaks. Hardest room to hold.
- Cinder already wants it hot. Easiest cure, if you can get them home.
