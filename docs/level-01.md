# Level 1 — The First Fire

## Layout

```
        [WOOD PILE]
            |
   [ORB 1]  |  [ORB 2]
            |
   +--------KILN--------+
   |                    |
   [ORB 3]             [ZOMBIE]
   +--------------------+
            |
       [EXIT / COURT]
```

## Objectives

1. Find the three orb people before they get infected.
2. Lure the zombie into the kiln to cure at least one.
3. Keep the kiln burning above 30 heat for 60 seconds.

## Starting Resources

- Heat: 60
- Wood: 3 units
- Orb people: 3 (all clean)
- Zombies: 1 (slow, patrols the south wall)

## Teaching Goals

- The kiln is the only safe zone.
- Zombies are medicine, not just monsters.
- Fuel is finite. Every rescue has a cost.

## Success Condition

At least 1 orb person saved, kiln still burning at end of timer.

## Failure Condition

All orb people turned, or heat hits 0.
